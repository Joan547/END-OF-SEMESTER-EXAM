About FutureMind AI Uganda

AI should not be only for big cities. Everyone in Uganda should learn it.

Who We Are

FutureMind AI Uganda is a technology organization based in Bamunanika, Luwero District. We teach students, teachers, farmers and business people how to use Artificial Intelligence to work smarter and build a better future.



Our Mission

To make AI simple, affordable and useful for every Ugandan.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every student and business uses AI to grow.

Our Values

Learning by doing, affordable fees, and helping our community.
